Changes by 言葉の森: merged spelling/reading candidates from the original
study list and OpenJLPT, verified pairs and intended English senses against
JMdict, corrected reviewed mappings, excluded unresolved candidates, removed
duplicate lexical sense identities, selected the intended matched study sense,
and retained full dictionary senses.
The original study list's source and licence were undocumented in this repo;
its English definitions are not included as definitions in this new catalog.
Dictionary definitions in this catalog come from the pinned JMdict snapshot.
Original list placement estimates are retained only for verified candidates
without an OpenJLPT placement. Reviewed overrides take precedence.

JLPT levels are community estimates, not official exam vocabulary coverage.
Priority labels are source dictionary markers, not app-measured frequency.
No OpenJLPT/Tatoeba example sentences or KANJIDIC2 data are included here.
